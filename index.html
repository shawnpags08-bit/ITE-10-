Why I Could Talk About Choral Singing for an Hour

By Shawn Louis G. Pagay

Introduction

This blog is about choral singing, my favorite hobby and one of the few things I can talk about without ever getting bored. I’ve been singing as a tenor for three years now, and in that time the choir has become much more than just an activity for me. I find choral music intriguing because it takes many different voices, each singing something different, and turns them into one sound. I still can’t get over that every time we rehearse.

Where It Started

When I first joined, I was shy and honestly embarrassed to sing in front of people. I didn’t know most of the songs, and I had zero training. There was even a time during Mass when my voice suddenly cracked in the middle of a song, and since my voice is loud, everyone heard it. It was so embarrassing that I wanted the floor to swallow me up. But I kept singing, and looking back, that moment taught me that mistakes are part of learning, and that nobody in a choir becomes good overnight.

What I Love About It

Today, I love almost everything about choir life: the harmonies when every voice part finally blends, the rehearsals, the performances, and the barkada I’ve gained along the way. As a tenor, I’m holding my own line while hearing the others around me, and when everything finally comes together, it’s honestly the best feeling.

Meeting the Philippine Madrigal Singers

One of my most memorable experiences was meeting the Philippine Madrigal Singers, also known as Madz. Founded in 1963 by Andrea Veneracion, they are one of the most respected choirs in the country, and they are my idols in singing. Last October 23, we had a workshop with them at CSU, where I learned so much just from being around them and seeing how they approach music.

The next day, October 24, was the grand choir for Among Halad, a benefit choral concert featuring the Madz. Many choirs from all over Caraga came together for a Madz concert, and we all got to sing a part of the concert together. Meeting people I had looked up to for so long made me incredibly happy. Not bad for someone who used to be too shy to sing.

What Choral Music Gave Me

Choral music has given me confidence, stress relief, friends, and discipline. Confidence, because I’m no longer the shy beginner. Stress relief, because after a long day, singing clears my head. Friends, because you can’t sing in harmony with people without becoming close. And discipline, because a choir only sounds good when everyone shows up, practices, and listens.

Final Thoughts

That’s why I chose choral singing. If you ever get the chance to join a choir, even if you’re yabag like I was, take it. You might end up with a hobby you can talk about for an hour, just like me.
